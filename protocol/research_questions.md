# Research Questions

## RQ1. Cue Convergence and Conflict

To what extent is the convergence or conflict of formal and semantic gender cues associated with gender-marking accuracy in L2 German?

## RQ2. Cue Dominance under Conflict

When formal and semantic gender cues conflict, to what extent do learners' gender-marking choices align with each cue's prediction?

## Supporting Analysis: Gender-Marking Consistency

Repeated uses of the same noun by individual learners will be examined to assess the stability of gender marking across grammatical contexts. This analysis will help evaluate whether observed patterns are more compatible with difficulties in lexical gender assignment or in agreement and morphological realization.

## Planned Analyses

- Gender-marking accuracy under cue convergence and conflict
- Gender choices aligned with formal versus semantic cues in conflict conditions
- Within-learner consistency across repeated noun occurrences
- Variation across grammatical contexts and learner proficiency

The analysis will account for differences across learners, nouns, and corpora. Corpus selection, coding criteria, and statistical models will be specified in the Stage 1 protocol.
